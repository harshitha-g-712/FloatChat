# How to Read FloatChat Oceanographic Charts

## 1. Vertical Depth Profile
- **Axes**: The horizontal x-axis shows the physical variable (Temperature in °C or Salinity in PSU). The vertical y-axis shows Pressure in dbar (proxy for depth), reversed so that 0 dbar is at the top (sea surface) and 2000 dbar is at the bottom (deep ocean).
- **Interpretation**: A steep horizontal shift over a narrow depth band marks the thermocline (temperature drop) or halocline (salinity increase). Multiple lines represent individual profiles.

## 2. Timeseries Chart
- **Axes**: The x-axis represents chronological time (months / years), and the y-axis indicates the parameter's measured value.
- **Interpretation**: Reveals seasonal warming/cooling cycles, monsoon freshening events, and multi-month trends.

## 3. Spatial Map
- **Axes**: Latitude (°N) and Longitude (°E).
- **Interpretation**: Displays geographical coordinates of profiling floats across the Bay of Bengal, Andaman Sea, and coastal boundaries.

## 4. Trajectory Map
- **Interpretation**: Connected chronological track showing the ocean current drift path taken by a robotic ARGO float over its operational lifetime.

## 5. Parameter Distribution (Histogram)
- **Axes**: Measured parameter value bins on x-axis vs. frequency/count of observations on y-axis.
- **Interpretation**: Shows median values, dispersion, outliers, and modal water mass characteristics.
