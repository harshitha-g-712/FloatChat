# Oceanographic Variables in ARGO Data

## Temperature (°C)
Ocean temperature measures the thermal heat content of seawater. In the Bay of Bengal, surface temperatures typically range between 27°C and 30°C due to intense solar radiation in the tropics. As depth increases, temperature decreases rapidly through the thermocline layer and stabilizes at 4°C to 6°C at depths below 1000 dbar.

## Salinity (PSU)
Salinity measures the concentration of dissolved mineral salts in seawater, expressed in Practical Salinity Units (PSU). 
- Normal open ocean salinity is approximately 34.5 to 35.5 PSU.
- In the Bay of Bengal, massive river runoff from the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Irrawaddy rivers during the monsoon season causes strong surface freshening, with surface salinity often dropping below 31.0 PSU.
- Below the surface layer (around 100 dbar), salinity increases sharply forming a pronounced halocline.

## Pressure (dbar) & Depth
Hydrostatic pressure is measured in decibars (dbar). In seawater, 1 dbar corresponds very closely to 1 meter of depth (e.g., 500 dbar ≈ 500 meters depth). ARGO profiling floats typically sample from the surface down to 2000 dbar.
