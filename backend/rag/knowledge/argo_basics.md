# ARGO Ocean Data Basics

ARGO is a global array of free-drifting profiling floats that measures the temperature and salinity of the upper 2000 m of the ocean. This allows continuous monitoring of the climate state of the ocean.

## Variables
- **Temperature**: Measured in °C. Reflects the heat content. Important for finding the thermocline (a steep temperature gradient in a body of water).
- **Salinity**: Measured in PSU (Practical Salinity Units). Helps identify different water masses. 
- **Pressure**: Measured in dbar. It is used as a proxy for depth (1 dbar is approximately 1 meter of depth).

## Bay of Bengal Regimes
The Bay of Bengal is heavily influenced by monsoons:
- **Southwest Monsoon (Summer)**: Brings heavy rainfall and river discharge, creating a very fresh (low salinity) surface layer.
- **Northeast Monsoon (Winter)**: Cool and dry air causes cooling of the sea surface.
Sub-regions include the Andaman Sea (eastern part) and the coastal margins.

## Charts
- **Depth Profile**: Shows how a variable changes with depth (pressure). The y-axis is reversed because pressure increases downwards.
- **Timeseries**: Shows how the average of a variable changes over months/years.
- **Map**: Shows the geographical distribution of floats.
