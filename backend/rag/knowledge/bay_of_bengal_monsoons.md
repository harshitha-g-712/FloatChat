# Bay of Bengal Monsoon Seasons & Dynamics

## Southwest Monsoon (June – September)
During the Summer or Southwest Monsoon, strong southwesterly winds drive heavy precipitation and trigger peak freshwater river discharges into the northern Bay of Bengal (Ganges-Brahmaputra and Irrawaddy river basins). This creates an extraordinarily fresh, low-salinity surface layer and intense vertical salinity stratification.

## Northeast Monsoon (November – February)
During the Winter or Northeast Monsoon, dry, cool winds blow from the Asian continent across the bay, causing surface evaporative cooling and wind-driven mixing. Surface waters cool down to ~25°C - 27°C, deepening the mixed layer.

## Spring & Fall Intermonsoon Transitions
- **Pre-monsoon (March – May)**: Maximum solar heating results in sea surface temperatures exceeding 29°C-30°C, fostering cyclone genesis.
- **Post-monsoon (October – November)**: Second period of active tropical cyclone formation with complex eddies and meandering boundary currents.
